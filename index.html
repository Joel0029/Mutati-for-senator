About Hon. Josphat Mutati

Hon. Josphat Mutati is a servant leader dedicated to transforming Kitui County through visionary leadership, integrity and accountability.


His mission is to champion the interests of every resident while promoting economic growth, quality education, better healthcare, youth empowerment and sustainable development.

Our Vision

To build a united, empowered and prosperous Kitui County where every citizen enjoys equal opportunities, quality services and sustainable economic growth.

Our Mission

To provide transparent leadership, protect devolution, advocate for quality healthcare and education, empower youth and women, improve agriculture and ensure every voice from Kitui County is represented.
